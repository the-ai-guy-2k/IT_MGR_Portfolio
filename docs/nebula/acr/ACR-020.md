## 7. Combined `#evidence` structure

`#evidence` remains one `section.narrative` with H2 **Credentials & Build-for-the-Team Evidence**, then nested `#credentials`, H3 **What sits behind the endpoint**, then `#project-evidence`.

This is a structural / labeling issue: one heading covers three different evidence types. Nested H3s keep the outline usable. **Not a material usability, accessibility, or visual-integrity defect.** No redesign in this ACI.

Recommended bounded correction (later ACI, if authorized): split into two or three sibling sections with their own H2s, or retitle the parent H2 so it does not fuse certifications with project evidence.
